# Evidence refresh adapters

Run `npx tsx scripts/collect-edgar-evidence.ts manifest.json --sources-verified` separately for each supported symbol. It appends investment evidence only. It never calls a trading endpoint or modifies account, positions, cash, trades, risk settings or migrations. This is an operator refresh command, not an enabled autonomous ingestion schedule.

Each manifest requires `symbol`, `managerCik`, and the existing `edgar` input (company CIK, CUSIP, current/prior filing references and periods, plus independently verified manager performance). Manager/security identity, corporate actions, original filings and comparable three-year benchmark outperformance must be reviewed before attesting. No shortlisted manager is automatically qualified. The refresh resolves the latest two original 13F-HR periods from that manager's SEC submissions, discovers the official information tables, and retains the approved performance evidence. Amendments, missing CUSIPs and nonconsecutive periods require review. It does not claim an absent security is a liquidation. Recent submissions only are supported; older history is not paged.

US financials use official SEC companyfacts, USD, 10-Q/10-K duration facts. Metrics within each cumulative period must share dates and accession. Discrete quarters are calculated from cumulative facts. Eight consecutive quarters provide current and previous TTM revenue, current TTM operating income, disclosed interest expense, operating cash and capex. A fresh comparable annual pair remains supported when newer quarterly data is absent. Missing or stale facts fail closed; no annual-only approximation substitutes for incomplete newer quarters. Full accession provenance is retained. Existing 120-day filing freshness is preserved, with a further 150-day reporting-end check.

BAE additionally requires `adrOrdinaryRatio: 4` and `baeReports`, an array of `{url, publishedAt, sha256}`. The separate adapter accepts independently verified official baesystems.com inline-XBRL reports only, checks their hashes and issuer identity, and extracts unsegmented GBP IFRS revenue, operating profit, disclosed InterestExpense, operating cash and property/plant/equipment purchases. It uses four consecutive half-years for comparative TTM, or a fresh annual comparison. FinanceCosts is deliberately not substituted for disclosed interest expense. PDF reports, custom taxonomy tags, unsupported number formats and missing IFRS metrics require further verified source work and remain ineligible. No live BAE report compatibility has been established. GBP financial ratios are not multiplied by the ADR ratio; market momentum must come from USD BAESY, matching the traded security.

The command needs TWELVE_DATA_API_KEY and an identifying SEC_USER_AGENT with a contact email. Market sources retained in evidence omit credentials. All collected evidence must pass the unchanged 60/40 rubric before insertion and expires within 24 hours. No manifests, qualifying investor performance or BAE source hashes were supplied with this change, and no evidence was imported. Complete live scores cannot be asserted until those inputs and provider connectivity are verified.
